KKP-RADAR · Research narrative

KKP-RADAR explores interpretable geometric and coherence-based representations of sensing data. The research began as a conceptual investigation of structured signal variation, with synthetic illustrations of intensity, gradients, curvature and phase.

The observatory now connects that research narrative to interactive educational demonstrations and publicly available environmental observations. Visitors can explore how different representations reveal different aspects of a scene, and compare synthetic examples with independently sourced weather and geospatial information.

The educational atlas uses synthetic examples. The live weather display presents published reflectivity products, not raw complex radar measurements. Neither establishes operational anomaly-detection performance for the proposed research architecture. Measured-data evaluation remains a separate research objective.

Public research abstract. The complete technical proposal, detailed implementation plans and private source materials are not reproduced here.
